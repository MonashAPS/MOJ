The panic button has been hit! Your firm is currently swiftly retreating from the market after the orderbook glitched and you've been called to help with the recovery. You are an analyst estimating losses to claim, and to solve this issue, you are given:

  • A sorted (ascending) array of integers nnn, representing the spread of last known orders.
  • A zero or positive integer kkk, representing your estimated capital.
  • A positive integer ccc, representing a range of risk values.

These represent the spread of the last known orders, estimated capital, and identify a range of risk values respectively. This risk value range is represented by the integer ccc, representing that the range encompasses the nc\frac{n}{c}cn​-th smallest number to the nc\frac{n}{c}cn​-th highest number in the array nnn. The integer kkk, your estimated capital, shall be used for operations on the values in the report you are writing up.

Each operation will cost 1 unit of your theoretical capital, but in exchange, you may increment or decrement any value in nnn by 1 (this will alter the risk range!). Your task is to maximize the risk range, namely, maximize the difference between the nc\frac{n}{c}cn​-th highest number and the nc\frac{n}{c}cn​-th smallest number in the array nnn, making only kkk operations.

Your company has told you, luckily, that the elements in the array nnn will always be a multiple of ccc, but that is all.

Good luck analyst, your company relies on you.
