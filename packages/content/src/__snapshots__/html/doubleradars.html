Ali recently found a treasure and has arranged the coins from the treasure around a circle. There are kkk houses around the circle with equal distances. Houses are numbered 111 through kkk consecutively in the clockwise direction. The treasure contains nnn coins, where the iiith coin (for 1≤i≤n1 \le i \le n1≤i≤n) has the value wiw_iwi​ and is located at the house xix_ixi​.

To protect the treasure, Ali has installed two radars stationed at the center of the circle, monitoring its circumference. Radar iii (for i∈{1,2}i \in \{1, 2\}i∈{1,2}) starts by monitoring house rir_iri​ and moves 1vi\frac{1}{v_i}vi​1​ houses per minute. More intuitively, every viv_ivi​ minutes, Radar iii goes one house forward. Initially, the first radar moves clockwise, and the second radar moves counterclockwise. Whenever the two radars meet, they both reverse their directions. Note that this can happen in the area between two adjacent houses.

Gholi, who wants to steal as many coins as possible, plans to start at an arbitrary house on the circle and move at most 1v\frac{1}{v}v1​ houses per minute in either direction (clockwise or counterclockwise). He starts moving at time zero. He can reverse his direction anytime or stay still for a while. If Gholi crosses paths with one of the radars at any moment, he will be immediately caught and sent to jail. He cannot steal a coin if this happens at a house.

Help Gholi to maximize the total value of the coins he can steal before being detected by the radars.

Input

The first line contains three integers, nnn (1≤n≤1051 \le n \le 10^51≤n≤105) the number of coins, kkk (1≤k≤1091 \le k \le 10^91≤k≤109) the number of houses around the circle, and vvv (1≤v≤1041 \le v \le 10^41≤v≤104) the speed of Gholi.

The second line contains the starting monitoring house r1r_1r1​ and speed v1v_1v1​ of the first radar (1≤r1≤k1 \le r_1 \le k1≤r1​≤k, 1≤v1≤1041 \le v_1 \le 10^41≤v1​≤104).

The third line contains the starting monitoring house r2r_2r2​ and speed v2v_2v2​ of the second radar (1≤r2≤k1 \le r_2 \le k1≤r2​≤k, 1≤v2≤1041 \le v_2 \le 10^41≤v2​≤104). It is guaranteed that r1≠r2r_1 \neq r_2r1​=r2​.

The fourth line contains nnn distinct integers, x1,x2,…,xnx_1, x_2, \ldots, x_nx1​,x2​,…,xn​, representing the houses where the coins are located (1≤xi≤k1 \le x_i \le k1≤xi​≤k).

The fifth line contains nnn integers, w1,w2,…,wnw_1, w_2, \ldots, w_nw1​,w2​,…,wn​, representing the value of each coin (1≤wi≤1091 \le w_i \le 10^91≤wi​≤109).

Output

Output the maximum total value of coins Gholi can steal before being detected by the radars.

Constraints

  • 1≤n≤1051 \le n \le 10^51≤n≤105
  • 1≤k≤1091 \le k \le 10^91≤k≤109
  • 1≤v,v1,v2≤1041 \le v, v_1, v_2 \le 10^41≤v,v1​,v2​≤104
  • 1≤r1,r2≤k1 \le r_1, r_2 \le k1≤r1​,r2​≤k and r1≠r2r_1 \neq r_2r1​=r2​
  • 1≤xi≤k1 \le x_i \le k1≤xi​≤k, all xix_ixi​ distinct
  • 1≤wi≤1091 \le w_i \le 10^91≤wi​≤109
