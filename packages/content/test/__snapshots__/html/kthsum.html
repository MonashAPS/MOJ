Given an array with nnn elements, what is the kkk-th largest sub array sum. (A subarray sum is the sum of all elements within a continuous section of the array).

Input

The first line contain integers nnn and kkk. The second line contains nnn integers a1,…,ana_1,\dots,a_na1​,…,an​, the elements of the array.

Output

Output the kkk-th largest subarray sum.

Constraints

  • 1≤n≤1041 \le n \le 10^41≤n≤104
  • 1≤k≤n(n+1)21 \le k \le \frac{n(n+1)}{2}1≤k≤2n(n+1)​
  • 1≤ai≤1091 \le a_i \le 10^91≤ai​≤109

Example 1

Input
10 1
7 7 4 6 3 8 9 8 5 1
Output
58

Example 2

Input
3 4
8 10 4
Output
10

Example 3

Input
5 6
3 6 9 8 4
Output
5 6
3 6 9 8 4
